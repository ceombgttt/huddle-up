**Last Updated: February 26, 2026**

Huddle Up ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our Platform at huddleupus.com.

**By using Huddle Up, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.**

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information:**
- Name
- Email address
- Password (encrypted)
- Phone number (optional)
- Gender (optional - for community building)

**Venue Information (for venue owners):**
- Business name and address
- Contact information
- Business license or proof of ownership
- Payment information (processed by third parties)

**User-Generated Content:**
- Watch party descriptions
- Comments and reviews
- Profile information
- Messages to other users
- Photos uploaded to parties

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Usage Data:**
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Operating system
- Pages viewed and links clicked
- Time spent on pages
- Referring website

**Location Data:**
- General location based on IP address
- Precise location (if you grant permission)

**Cookies and Tracking:**
- Session cookies
- Persistent cookies
- Analytics cookies
- Advertising cookies (third-party)

We use your information to:

**Provide Services:**
- Create and manage your account
- Display watch parties and venue listings
- Process payments (via third-party processors)
- Verify venue ownership
- Send service-related notifications
- Enable QR code check-ins
- Show predictions and leaderboards

**Improve Platform:**
- Analyze usage patterns
- Fix bugs and improve features
- Develop new features
- Conduct research and analytics

**Marketing and Communication:**
- Send promotional emails (you can opt out)
- Notify you of new features
- Share updates about sports events
- Send newsletters
- SMS/Push notifications for parties

**Legal and Safety:**
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect our rights and property

### 4.1 Public Information

**The following information is PUBLIC and visible to all users:**

- Your gender (if provided, shown to party attendees)

- Venue information for claimed venues
- Reviews and comments you post

**Service Providers:**
- Payment processors (Stripe)
- Hosting providers (Replit, Vercel)

- Email services (SendGrid, Mailchimp)
- SMS services (Twilio)
- Customer support tools

**Legal Requirements:**
- Law enforcement (if required by law)
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Protection of rights and safety

**Business Transfers:**
- In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

### 4.3 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

**We do not sell your personal information to third parties.**

However, we may share aggregated, anonymized data for research or marketing purposes.

## 5. DATA RETENTION

We retain your information:
- **Account data:** Until you delete your account, plus 90 days
- **Payment records:** 7 years (for tax/legal compliance)
- **Usage logs:** 2 years
- **Deleted accounts:** Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely
